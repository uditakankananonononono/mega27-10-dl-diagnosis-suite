\section{Reproducibility}
Commit-pinned code, hermetic pytest suite (13 tests, no network), fixed
seeds, committed splits and probability files. Every headline metric is
computed twice with independent implementations (scikit-learn and
torchmetrics) over the committed probability files; all eight agree
exactly (\texttt{results/metrics\_crossverify.json}). Layer-level
architecture dumps (torchinfo) are committed as
\texttt{results/model\_architectures.txt}.
