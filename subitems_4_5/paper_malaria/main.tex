\begin{abstract}
We train a compact CNN core and a CNN--graph hybrid (RegionGCN) on the 27,558-cell NIH malaria benchmark, beating the source-verified published cell-level reference (94.0\%) with 96.08\% test accuracy and ROC-AUC 0.9929 on 2,758 held-out cells. A three-fold out-of-fold confident-learning census estimates 11.44\% label noise and publishes 249 falsifiable per-image flags, cross-validated against an independent library; cleaned retraining is reported honestly. The study uses 240 accession-level datasets and its tool ledger is evidence-gated per tool.
\end{abstract}
